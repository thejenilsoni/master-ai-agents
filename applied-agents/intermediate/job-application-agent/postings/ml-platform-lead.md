# Engineering Manager, ML Platform

**Company:** Verdance AI
**Location:** London, UK (on site)

We are hiring a manager to lead the team that builds and runs our model-training
and serving infrastructure.

## Requirements

- 4+ years managing engineers directly, including performance reviews and hiring
- 3+ years running Kubernetes in production at multi-cluster scale
- Deep experience with GPU scheduling and distributed training
- Experience owning an ML feature store and model registry
- Strong Go or Rust for the platform's control plane
- Track record setting technical direction across multiple teams

## Nice to have

- PyTorch internals
- Experience with Ray or Kubeflow
- Published work on ML systems

## About the role

You would manage six engineers and report to the VP of Engineering. This is a
people-management role; roughly 70% of your week is spent on people and
planning, not code.
