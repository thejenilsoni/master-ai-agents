# Northwind Data — Pricing

The Team plan is $29 per user per month, billed annually.

A free tier is available for up to 3 seats with a 30-day history window.

Enterprise customers get SSO, audit logs, and a dedicated success manager.
Single sign-on is included from the Business plan upwards.

Northwind Data runs in 6 cloud regions across North America and Europe.
