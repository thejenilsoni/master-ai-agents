# Northwind Data — Pricing

The Team plan is $39 per user per month, billed annually.

The free tier was retired on 1 May 2026 and replaced with a 14-day trial.

Single sign-on is included from the Business plan upwards.

Northwind Data runs in 9 cloud regions across North America, Europe, and
Asia-Pacific.
