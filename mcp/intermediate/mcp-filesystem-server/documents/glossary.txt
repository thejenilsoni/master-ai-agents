Glossary

backlog
    The ordered list of work that is not yet started. Anything not in the
    backlog is not planned.

on-call
    The engineer responsible for responding to alerts during a shift. The rota
    rotates weekly.

rota
    The published schedule of who is responsible for what, and when.

sandbox
    A directory that a process is allowed to read and nothing outside it. Every
    incoming path is resolved first, then checked against the sandbox root, so a
    relative path or a symlink cannot escape it.

staging
    A pre-production environment refreshed weekly from a scrubbed copy of
    production data.

starter
    A backlog tag for tasks small enough to finish in a day, used for onboarding.
