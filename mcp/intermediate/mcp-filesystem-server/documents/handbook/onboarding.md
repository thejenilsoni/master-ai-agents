# Onboarding

Welcome to the team. Your first week has three goals: get your environment
running, ship one small change, and meet the people you will work with most.

## Day one

- Collect your accounts from the operations rota.
- Clone the service repository and run the test suite locally.
- Read the style guide before opening your first pull request.

## Day two to four

- Pick a task tagged `starter` from the backlog.
- Pair with your buddy for the first review round.
- Write down anything that confused you — that list becomes your first
  documentation contribution.

## Day five

- Demo what you shipped at the Friday round-up.
- Book a short chat with each person on the rota.

## Environments

We run three environments: `local`, `staging`, and `production`. Local runs
entirely on your machine with seeded data, so nothing you do there can affect a
real customer. Staging mirrors production but is refreshed weekly.
