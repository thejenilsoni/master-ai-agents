# Aurora Notes — Support & Policies

## Refund policy
Paid plans can be cancelled at any time. If you cancel within 14 days of your
first payment, you get a full refund. After 14 days, your plan stays active
until the end of the current billing period; no partial refunds are issued.

## How do I export my notes?
Go to Settings → Export. You can export a single notebook or your entire account
as Markdown, PDF, or an encrypted `.aurora` archive. Exports are generated
on-device and never pass through Aurora servers unencrypted.

## Support response times
- Free plan: community forum support.
- Plus plan: email support, replies within 2 business days.
- Team plan: priority email support, replies within 4 business hours, plus a
  dedicated onboarding call.

## Account recovery
If you lose your password, you can reset it by email. If you also lose your
end-to-end encryption recovery phrase, Aurora cannot recover your encrypted
notes — the recovery phrase is the only key. Store it somewhere safe.

## Data retention after deletion
When you delete your account, encrypted note data is removed from active systems
immediately and purged from encrypted backups within 30 days.
