# Aurora Notes — Product FAQ

## What is Aurora Notes?
Aurora Notes is a cross-platform note-taking app with offline sync, end-to-end
encryption, and an AI search that answers questions across all your notes.

## Which platforms are supported?
Aurora Notes runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, and Android. A web app is
available at app.auroranotes.example.

## What are the plans and prices?
- **Free**: up to 3 notebooks and 500 notes. Offline sync on 2 devices.
- **Plus** ($6/month): unlimited notebooks and notes, sync on unlimited devices,
  and version history for 30 days.
- **Team** ($12/user/month): everything in Plus, shared notebooks, admin
  controls, and version history for 1 year.

## Is my data encrypted?
Yes. Notes are end-to-end encrypted by default. Aurora Notes cannot read your
note contents. Encryption keys never leave your devices unless you enable
encrypted cloud backup with a recovery phrase.

## Does AI search send my notes to a third party?
On the Free and Plus plans, AI search runs against an encrypted index and only
the specific snippets needed to answer a query are decrypted on-device. Team
plans can optionally route AI search through a self-hosted model.
