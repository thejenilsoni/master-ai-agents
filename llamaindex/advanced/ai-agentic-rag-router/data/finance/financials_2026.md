# Nimbus Cloud — 2026 Financial Summary

## Revenue
- Q1 2026: $4.2M total revenue.
- Q2 2026: $4.8M total revenue.
- H1 2026 revenue was therefore $9.0M, up 22% year over year.

Roughly 70% of revenue comes from the Pro plan, 25% from Enterprise, and the
remainder from usage overages.

## Costs and margin
- Gross margin in H1 2026 was **78%**, up from 74% a year earlier, driven by
  cheaper edge bandwidth and better autoscaling utilization.
- Infrastructure (cloud compute, storage, bandwidth) is the largest cost of
  revenue at about 18% of revenue.
- Sales & marketing was 31% of revenue; R&D was 24%.

## Customers
- 3,100 paying customers at the end of Q2 2026.
- Net revenue retention was 116%, meaning existing customers expanded spend
  faster than churn reduced it.

## Outlook
Management guides to $10.5M+ revenue in H2 2026 and expects gross margin to hold
near 78% as Enterprise mix grows.
