# Nimbus Cloud — Product Overview

## Plans
- **Starter** — $0/month. 1 project, 5 GB storage, community support. 99.5%
  uptime target (no financial SLA).
- **Pro** — $49/month per project. 500 GB storage, email support, and a
  contractual **99.9% uptime SLA** with service credits.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing (typically $2,000+/month). Unlimited storage,
  a dedicated account manager, SSO, and a **99.99% uptime SLA**.

## Core features
Nimbus Cloud is a managed platform for deploying containerized apps: git-push
deploys, autoscaling, managed Postgres, and global edge caching. Pro and
Enterprise add private networking and audit logs.

## Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- Pro: 99.9% monthly uptime. Below that, customers receive service credits of
  10% (below 99.9%) up to 50% (below 99.0%) of the monthly fee.
- Enterprise: 99.99% monthly uptime with credits up to 100% of the monthly fee,
  plus a 30-minute support response time for Sev-1 incidents.

## Support response times
Starter: community forum. Pro: email, 1 business day. Enterprise: 30 minutes for
Sev-1, 4 business hours otherwise.
