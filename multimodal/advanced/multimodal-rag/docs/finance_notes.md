# Finance review notes

## Scope
This note accompanies the quarterly revenue chart prepared for the internal
finance review. It records commentary only. **The per-quarter figures live in the
chart itself and are deliberately not repeated here**, which is how the finance
team avoids two copies of the same number drifting apart.

## Commentary
Growth through the year was driven by Enterprise expansion rather than new
logos. The strongest quarter was noticeably ahead of the surrounding ones, and
the following quarter gave back part of that gain as two large renewals slipped
into the next period.

## Gross margin
Gross margin held near 78% across the year. Infrastructure remains the largest
cost of revenue at roughly 18%.
